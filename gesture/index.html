Gripper Gesture Study

Short clips of a robot arm (WidowX) moving a red drawer. You judge how the gripper moves, not whether the task succeeds.

What to judge

Each clip shows one moment where the drawer moves. You are told what the robot was asked to do (open or close the drawer) and when the drawer moves. Watch the gripper during that window and answer:

  • Clean: it moves smoothly with the drawer, in the direction the task asks.
  • Messy: something is clearly off. Tick what you see: it twists (turns around its own axis), slips (slides along the handle, comes off it, drifts sideways or up and down), jerks (stop-and-go, shaking), or pushes or pulls the wrong way for the task.
  • Can't tell: the gripper is hidden or the clip is too unclear.

Clips loop and play at half speed; you can change the speed. Start with 6 practice clips that show the expected answer, then the study clips. Everything is saved as you go; you can stop and come back with the same name.
