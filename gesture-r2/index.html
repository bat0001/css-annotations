The clips you could not label with one answer. Describe them over time instead.

What to do

For each clip, mark the moments that matter: pause at the start of a moment, click Start: now, move to its end, click End: now, choose what the gripper does, and click Add. Add as many moments as you need (for example: clean, then twists, then pushes). Then describe the clip in a sentence. If you still cannot tell, tick Still can't tell.

The green band under the video is the window where the drawer moves. Whole episode shows the full clip for context; times are kept consistent between the two views.
