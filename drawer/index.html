Robot Drawer Annotation

Short clips of a small robot arm working on a red drawer, with a black handle, in a small white box. Each clip lasts about seven seconds. For every clip, answer four questions about what you see happen to the drawer and the gripper. There is no expected answer and no trick: report what you see, and use Unsure whenever the clip does not let you tell.

Your answers are saved automatically under this name. Come back later with the same name to pick up where you left off. If someone else might use the same first name, add an initial.
Question 1

Grasp

Do the gripper's fingers close on the drawer's handle at some point in the clip? Yes, firmly: it closes and holds. Yes, but poorly: it closes but holds loosely, slips along the handle, or lets go and grasps again. Touching the box or the drawer front without closing on the handle is No.

Question 2

Drawer motion

While the gripper holds the handle or touches the drawer, does the drawer open (slide out of the box), close (slide back in), or not move? Judge the drawer, not the arm.

Question 3

Pull or push

While it holds the handle or touches the drawer, does the gripper pull out (away from the box), push in (toward the box), move another way (sideways, up, down), or show no clear motion? If it never touches the drawer: Does not touch.

Question 4

Clean or messy

While it holds the handle or touches the drawer, does the gripper move cleanly (smoothly, mostly in one direction) or is it messy (jerks, twists, swerves, moves in several directions)? Judge the gripper's motion, not whether the drawer opens. If it never touches the drawer: Does not touch.

Tricky cases

  • The drawer opens, but the robot is all over the place. Answer Questions 1 to 3 as usual (the drawer Opens; for Question 3 give the gripper's main direction) and Question 4 Messy.
  • The fingers never close on the handle, but the gripper pushes or pulls the drawer (open fingers, the drawer's edge, a knock). Question 1 is No; Questions 2 to 4 describe what happens while the gripper touches the drawer. Example: it pushes the drawer in without grasping → No / Closes (or Does not move if the drawer stays put) / Pushes in / Clean or Messy.
  • The gripper grasps badly: it holds loosely, slips along the handle, or lets go and grasps again. Question 1 Yes, but poorly; for Questions 2 and 3 the first clear movement still counts.
  • The gripper never touches the drawer at all. Question 1 No, Question 2 Does not move, Questions 3 and 4 Does not touch.
  • The drawer opens and is then pushed back in (or the reverse). For Questions 2 and 3, describe the first clear movement.
  • You cannot see (the arm hides the drawer, it goes too fast): answer Unsure. It is a valid answer, not a failure.
  • Each clip loops. Use Slow to play it at half speed.
